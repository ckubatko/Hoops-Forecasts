Data

Forecasts come from the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) once tip-off is within 7 days. Earlier games show example conditions, marked as such. History uses ERA5 reanalysis through Open-Meteo.

Reading the map

Each city is a station model: the circle fills with cloud cover, the barb points into the wind (half barb 5 kt, full barb 10 kt), temperature is upper left and dew point lower left.
